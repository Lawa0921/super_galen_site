Act I — The Actor

In Silence,
I Speak

I am AC, an actor born in Taiwan and now based in New York City. Growing between cultures shaped my perspective and the way I perform. From Taipei's lanes to Manhattan's stages, every crossing reminds me that identity is not one label, but countless stories layered together.

(A pause. The spotlight narrows.)

I am not a household name, and I do not live a Hollywood dream. I am committed to the craft of acting and to telling meaningful stories. New York is where I keep training, auditioning, collaborating, and making room for the work I love.

(He turns to face the audience.)

As a SAG-AFTRA member, I move between independent films, television, and the stage. Acting is not about volume, but truth. Every role is a chance to prove, in each silence, that something does not need to be seen to be felt.

Act III — The Craft

What I Bring to the Stage

Screen Acting

From HBO's Succession to the independent short Breaking the Silence, I bring tension, silence, and conflict to the camera. Lead or supporting, every role deserves to be lived fully.

p.1

Stunt Coordination

Movement has a voice, too. My experience as a stunt coordinator on Succession taught me to find the balance between safety and truth. The body is the most honest storytelling tool.

p.2

Cross-Cultural Bridge

Born in Taiwan, shaped in New York. I move naturally between Eastern and Western performance languages and understand emotional expression across cultures. That makes me a distinct asset in cross-cultural productions.

p.3

Cinema Advocate

Cinema is more than work; it is a belief. From del Toro's Frankenstein to David Archuleta's honest confession, I keep exploring how images move us and let quiet emotion be heard on screen.
